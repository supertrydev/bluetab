## If you publish a fork

1. Choose your own name.
2. Replace the icons in `src/assets/` and the store images.
3. Change the Firefox add-on ID in `src/platform/firefox-manifest.ts` and the
   `key` in `manifest.json`.
4. Point the account, sync and AI settings in `src/config/config.ts` to your own
